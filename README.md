# Life Compass

A calm, minimal planner and journal that works offline and installs to your phone's home screen.

- **Plan**: tasks for today, the week, month, year or someday, plus a "coming up" list.
- **Review**: mood, daily rituals and three short prompts for each day, week, month or year.
- **Growth**: your life map, a mind map you can present as slides.
- **Reflect**: quick journaling, notes, ideas and mind maps. Turn any page into a map with one tap.

Everything is saved in your browser. You can also sync across devices through a private GitHub gist (Settings → Sync).
